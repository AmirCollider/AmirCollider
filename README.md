<summary><b>🇬🇧 &nbsp;English</b> &nbsp;·&nbsp; <i>a little about me</i> ⋆˙⟡</summary>

<br>

I'm **AmirCollider** — an indie dev from Iran. I make small games, the tools that
came out of making them, and printable workbooks for learning Japanese. INFP, so
every project starts as a feeling and ends as a settings window nobody asked for `(๑˃ᴗ˂)ﻭ`

Everything I ship speaks **Persian, English and Japanese**, and Persian is
right-to-left — which is how one of these tools happened at all.

**🎮 Games**

| | what it is | where |
|---|---|---|
| 🌸 | **Neon Katana** — a neon Fruit Ninja-style game, with online duels | [page](https://amircollider.com/en/neon-katana) · [APK](https://amircollider.com/neon-katana/download?store=apk) |
| 🗡 | **Chrono Blades** — one knife, one spinning target, a stage that never ends | [page](https://amircollider.com/en/chronoblades) · [APK](https://dl.amircollider.com/APK/ChronoBlades.apk) |
| 🏓 | **Crazy Pong** — neon pong in your browser, nothing to install | [play](https://amircollider.com/en/crazypong) |
| 🧵 | **Frayed Red String** — a psychological visual novel; the demo is out for PC and Android | [page](https://amircollider.com/en/the-frayed-red-string) |

**🧰 Tools**

| | what it is | where |
|---|---|---|
| 🎙 | **Vocana** — write plain English, hear it in any VOICEVOX voice, with captions and word timing | [page](https://amircollider.com/en/vocana) · [repo](https://github.com/AmirCollider/Vocana) |

| 🖋 | **Unity DirectTMP** — hand TextMeshPro the font file itself, and every language just works | [page](https://amircollider.com/en/unity-directtmp) · [repo](https://github.com/AmirCollider/UnityDirectTMP) · [OpenUPM](https://openupm.com/packages/com.amircollider.unitydirecttmp/) |

**📖 Books** — printable A4 workbooks, one page a day, in English and Persian editions

| | what it is | where |
|---|---|---|
| あ | **Hiragana Day by Day** — from your first letter to your first sentences in 30, 60 or 90 days | [book](https://amircollider.com/en/books/hiragana) · [free sample](https://amircollider.com/en/books/hiragana/sample) |
| ア | **Katakana Day by Day** — the same path, for katakana | [book](https://amircollider.com/en/books/katakana) |

**Vocana** and **DirectTMP** are free (DirectTMP is MIT), and **DocSnap** has a free
edition that needs no key and no account — you can
[click around a real export](https://amircollider.com/en/unity-docsnap) before installing
anything. Every letter of the books has a free stroke-order page at
[amircollider.com/books](https://amircollider.com/en/books).

💌 &nbsp;[amircollider.com/contact](https://amircollider.com/en/contact) — I read and answer these myself.

**AmirCollider** です。イランの個人開発者で、小さなゲームと、その制作中に生まれた
ツール、そして日本語を学ぶための印刷できるワークブックをつくっています。INFP なので、
